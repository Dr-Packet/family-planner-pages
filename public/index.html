Family Planner

A home dashboard for organizing family calendars, routines, chores, rewards, allowances, and shared household planning.

Family Planner is a self-hosted application used by authorized household members. The application is not offered as an open public service. This website explains its features and privacy practices.

Read Privacy PolicyTerms of Use

What Family Planner does

  • Display household schedules and selected Google Calendar events
  • Organize recurring chores and routines
  • Track household rewards and allowances
  • Help authorized household members coordinate shared activities

Google sign-in

Authorized users can sign in using Google account identity information, such as their name, email address, and a Google account identifier, to establish and manage access to Family Planner.

Google Calendar connection

Users can grant Family Planner access to selected Google Calendar information for the household scheduling interface. Calendar information appears to people who have access to the shared household dashboard. See the Privacy Policy for the types of information processed and how to disconnect an account.

Designed for planning, not AI image generation

Family Planner is not an artificial-intelligence image generation or editing service. Google account and Calendar information is used to provide authentication and household planning features, not to create or distribute intimate imagery or to train generalized AI models.
