Family Planner

Family Planner is a private household planning application for routines, chores, allowance tracking, rewards, and shared family schedules.

It is designed for families who want a simple, organized place to coordinate day-to-day responsibilities and important events without making the service publicly accessible.

Privacy Policy Terms

What it helps with

  • Shared family schedules and recurring events
  • Household routines and daily planning
  • Chores, rewards, and accountability
  • Allowance tracking and family coordination
  • Private access for household members

Why Google sign-in is used

Google sign-in is used for secure authentication and user identification in the private Family Planner application. It allows authorized household users to access the system with a familiar Google account sign-in flow rather than creating a separate account system for the app itself.

How Google Calendar is used

Google Calendar integration is used for authorized calendar functionality related to shared family scheduling, reminders, and recurring household events. The app can use Google Calendar access to view or manage events relevant to household coordination, as configured by the application owner.

Application purpose

Family Planner is a household planning application designed to help a family organize routines, chores, rewards, allowance tracking, and shared schedules in one private system.

This website provides public documentation, legal notices, and Google verification information for the project. It does not provide a public Family Planner login or a publicly accessible app instance.

Google APIs are used for authentication and authorized calendar functionality. Family Planner is not an AI imagery application, and it does not generate, modify, or distribute AI-generated intimate imagery.
