Privacy Policy

Effective: October 14, 2026 · Application: Family Planner · Website: blackholesec.tech

Who operates the app. Family Planner is a private, self-hosted household planning application operated by the household administrator. This public website is the official information and policy site for the Family Planner application. For privacy questions and deletion requests, contact privacy@blackholesec.tech.

What this covers. This policy covers the private Family Planner application, its Google sign-in and Google Calendar features, and the public informational site. It tells you what information is accessed, how it is used and shared, how long it is kept, and how you can revoke access or request deletion.

1. Information accessed and collected

Data categoryWhat it includesWhy it is processed

Connecting Google Calendar requires a separate Google authorization. The Google consent screen identifies the permissions being requested. Family Planner does not receive your Google password. It does not request Google data for unrelated services such as Gmail or Google Drive unless you separately authorize a feature that specifically requires it and this policy is updated first.

2. How Google user information is used

  • Authentication: to recognize an authorized household account and provide a sign-in session.
  • Calendar display: to retrieve and show events from calendars deliberately connected by the user.
  • Calendar refresh: to update displayed scheduling information while a connection remains authorized.
  • Security and troubleshooting: to resolve failures or investigate unauthorized access where necessary.

Family Planner uses Google user data only to provide or improve these user-facing features. It does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising or data brokerage, or use Google Workspace API data to develop, improve or train generalized or non-personalized artificial intelligence or machine-learning models. Google user data is not used to generate, modify or distribute non-consensual intimate imagery.

3. Who can see and receive the information

Household members: Events from connected calendars that appear on a shared Family Planner display can be seen by people with access to that household dashboard. Only connect calendars whose contents you are comfortable sharing with those household members.

Service providers: Google processes authentication and Calendar requests. The public informational site is served using GitHub Pages, which can process technical requests for the pages it hosts according to GitHub's policies. Private-network and hosting providers may process limited connection metadata needed to provide network connectivity. The public site is not used to store the household's Google OAuth tokens or calendar events.

Other disclosures: We do not sell or rent personal data, share it with advertising platforms, or give calendar content to unrelated data brokers. Data may be disclosed when authorized by the user, necessary to provide the displayed household functions, or legally required.

4. Storage and security

Family Planner is operated on a privately managed, self-hosted system. Connected-account settings, household entries and any persistent Google connection tokens are handled by that private application rather than by this public GitHub Pages site. Calendar information is retrieved for use in the private scheduling interface and can be held locally to support calendar display and synchronization.

Access to the private installation is limited through account authorization and private network controls, including Tailscale connectivity. We take reasonable steps to restrict who can reach the application and its stored data, maintain access controls for the host, and protect OAuth credentials from publication in this website's source code. No technical security measure guarantees complete protection.

5. Data retention: how long information is kept

Connected Google account data: Google account identifiers, connection details and tokens are needed only while an account is authorized for the private application. Calendar data is retained or refreshed as needed for the household's calendar view. Access should be disabled when an account is disconnected.

Household entries: Tasks, schedules, routines, rewards and allowance entries remain in the private household application until they are removed by an authorized administrator or a verified deletion request is completed.

Deletion requests: After confirming the request concerns an account you control, the household administrator will remove the relevant Google connection and associated locally stored account/calendar data from active application storage within 30 days. Other household records tied to that person will be removed where appropriate and technically feasible. Any copies in routine backups are removed as those backups expire, or earlier when practicable. Legal or security obligations may require retaining limited records for longer.

6. Your choices, account disconnection and deletion

You can revoke Family Planner's Google permissions at any time from Google Account → Third-party connections. This stops further authorized Google API access, but it does not automatically guarantee deletion of information that was already saved locally by the private application.

To request access to, correction of, or deletion of locally stored information, email privacy@blackholesec.tech. State which Google account is involved and whether you want the Calendar connection removed, the associated personal data deleted, or both. Do not email OAuth tokens or account passwords. We may ask for enough information to verify that you control the relevant account. See the Data Deletion page for the step-by-step process.

7. Children and family accounts

Family Planner is a tool for households, including adults managing schedules and routines involving children. Household administrators are responsible for deciding what information about children is entered and who can view it. The administrator should use an appropriate adult-managed Google connection rather than asking a child to authorize unrelated Google account access.

8. Public website

The public website at blackholesec.tech contains informational pages; it is not where household calendars, account data or Google tokens are stored. We do not include advertising trackers or first-party analytics scripts on these pages. GitHub, the hosting provider, may process routine request metadata as described in its own privacy statement.

9. Policy changes

We will update this policy when the application's data practices change. If a change introduces materially new uses of Google user data, affected users will be informed and asked for any required consent before the new use begins. The effective date above shows when this version was published.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, account-data requests, correction or deletion: privacy@blackholesec.tech.
