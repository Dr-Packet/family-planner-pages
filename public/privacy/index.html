Privacy Policy

This privacy policy explains how Family Planner may use Google login and Google Calendar data for the private family dashboard described on this website.

1. Information we may receive from Google

Family Planner may use Google authentication to allow a user to sign in with their Google account. This may include the user’s Google account identity information needed to confirm access, such as a name, email address, and basic profile data required by Google’s sign-in service.

If the application is configured to access Google Calendar, it may also receive access to calendar data that is explicitly authorized by the user through Google’s consent flow. Access is limited to the permissions the app requests and the user approves.

2. How data is used

Family Planner may use Google account information and calendar data for ordinary app functionality, such as:

  • authenticating users to the private application
  • displaying calendar events relevant to family scheduling
  • helping keep household tasks and shared events organized

This public GitLab Pages site does not collect or process personal data for analytics or advertising. It is a static landing page intended for public documentation and Google verification.

3. Storage

The public GitLab Pages site stores only static content such as this page and related documents. The actual Family Planner application and any user or calendar data remain on the private self-hosted system and are not stored in this public repository or Pages site unless the owner specifically configures such storage elsewhere.

Any data actually stored by the private app, including Google account information or calendar data, is controlled by the owner of the private application and should be documented in the app’s own privacy notice.

4. Retention

The public website does not track or retain visitor analytics. The owner should define retention periods for any personally identifiable data kept in the private application, such as Google account records, calendar entries, or household information.

5. Sharing

Family Planner does not sell personal information. Information may be shared only with Google as required for authentication or calendar permissions, or with the owner’s chosen hosting or infrastructure providers if those services are used to run the private application.

This public site itself does not share personal data with third-party analytics or advertising providers.

6. Deletion and access

Users who wish to remove or revoke Google access for the private app should do so through their Google Account permissions settings. The private app owner should also provide a process for deleting any family data stored by the app and for responding to user requests.

7. Manual owner actions required

This website and privacy template are intentionally simple. The owner must confirm and complete the following before publishing a live app:

  • exact Google OAuth and Google Calendar scopes used by the app
  • where the private app stores user and calendar data
  • the retention period for any stored data
  • how account deletion and data removal requests are handled
  • the exact domain and pages to be used in Google Console

8. Contact

For questions about this project or its privacy practices, the owner should provide a contact method in the private application or on a final product site. This public pages repository does not provide user-facing account support.

This is a minimal public policy template. The owner should tailor it to the final implementation of Family Planner before public launch.
