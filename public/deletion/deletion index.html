Data Deletion and Google Account Disconnection

Effective: October 14, 2026

Family Planner is operated for an authorized household. You control whether your Google account remains connected and can ask the administrator to remove Google account data stored by Family Planner.

1. Stop future access to your Google account

  1. Open Google Account → Third-party connections.
  2. Find the Family Planner connection and remove access.
  3. Revocation prevents future authorized API access but does not, by itself, delete previously synced information from the household's private system.

2. Request removal of locally stored data

  1. Email privacy@blackholesec.tech with the subject Family Planner Data Deletion.
  2. Identify the Google account email address associated with the connection and specify whether to remove the Google connection, locally stored calendar data, other personal household records, or all applicable data.
  3. The household administrator may verify account ownership before processing the request. Do not send passwords, OAuth tokens, or private event contents.
  4. Relevant locally stored account and calendar information is removed from active application storage within 30 days of verification. Routine backups expire under the administrator's normal backup rotation schedule.

3. What may be affected

Deleting linked-account records can remove the ability to refresh Google Calendar events and may remove cached information from the household calendar display. Deleting household entries such as chores, routines and rewards can affect shared household records. Data in your original Google Calendar remains in your Google account unless you delete it using Google's own controls.

4. Questions

Contact privacy@blackholesec.tech. Read the full Privacy Policy.
